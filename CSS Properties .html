Arthur knew the heartbeat of every machine in the room. He could tell, just by the specific rhythm of a tick, if a mainspring was wound too tight or if a gear tooth had worn thin. He took comfort in their precision. In a world that felt increasingly chaotic and rushed, the clocks were predictable. They measured time perfectly, second by second, never looking back.
